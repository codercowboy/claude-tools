Inflation Calculator

See how the buying power of the US dollar changes over time. Enter an amount, a from year, and a to year — everything runs locally in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Equivalent amount. What your amount from the "from" year would be worth in the "to" year, scaled by the change in the price index.
  • Cumulative inflation. The total percentage change in prices across the whole span.
  • Average annual rate. The steady yearly rate (compounded) that produces that same overall change.
  • Swap (⇄). Flip the two years to look backward instead of forward.
  • Copy (📋). The amount and the result each have a copy-to-clipboard button.

The tool uses the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI-U annual-average series (all urban consumers, U.S. city average, all items; 1982-84=100), bundled into the page and covering . It is public-domain U.S. Government data. Figures are nominal-dollar comparisons and reflect the national average basket, not any one region or household.

Your amount and year selections are remembered on this device, so reopening the tool picks up where you left off.
