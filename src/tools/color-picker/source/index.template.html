    How Color Picker works

    Load an image — click Load image to choose a file, or drag and drop one onto the canvas — then scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag to pan around it (Reset view returns to the fit-to-frame view).

    • Sample a pixel. Turn on 🔍 Choose Color, then click a spot on the image (or drag to aim the crosshair and tap, on touch) — the magnifier loupe zooms in so you can land on the exact pixel.
    • Collected colors. Each sample adds a row below with its swatch, rgba() value, and hex value — copy either one, or remove the row with the trash icon.
    • Remove all. Clears every sampled color, after a confirmation.
    • Copy all. The RGBA and HEX boxes list every sampled color, one per line — use "Copy all" under each to grab the whole list at once.

    Your sampled colors are saved on this device, so they're still here next time you open Color Picker.
